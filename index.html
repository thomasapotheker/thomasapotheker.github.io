Research project / Language models

SIDReasoner

Reproducing SIDReasoner: Reasoning over Semantic IDs

We reproduced and extended a generative recommender that represents catalogue items as semantic IDs. Our experiments examined reinforcement learning, decoding constraints, and the value of generating reasoning at inference time.

My contribution focused on designing and running the Office Products experiments, including reward, rollout, LoRA, and reasoning ablations, and contributing to the report.

Alexandra Antonica, Thomas Apotheker, Erin Elagöz, and Mattia Sferrazza

Research project / Computer vision

Exploring sparse mechanisms for WSI classification

Efficient deep learning for whole-slide pathology images

We compared sparse attention, state-space models, and multiple-instance learning approaches on CAMELYON16, studying the trade-off between classification accuracy, GPU memory, and computation time.

The experiments found that sparse methods can retain competitive accuracy at lower computational cost, while simpler aggregators were more robust when feature resolution was reduced.
